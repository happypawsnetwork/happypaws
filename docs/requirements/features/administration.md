# Administration

The web administration panel serves as the operational headquarters for Happy Paws. Administrators oversee platform safety, manage user verifications, and coordinate rescue efforts.

## Dashboard & Coordination
- ❌ **Live case map**: A real-time map plots all active rescue cases, allowing administrators to monitor AI-assigned urgency, transporter progress, and case resolution.
- ✅ **Reporting dashboard**: A centralized web dashboard provides a summary of pending verifications, open cases, and overall platform activity.

## Identity & Content Moderation
- ❌ **KYC review**: Administrators inspect submitted identity documents, clinic registrations, and professional licenses. They must explicitly approve or reject these submissions before accounts gain full role-based privileges.
- ❌ **Content moderation**: Administrators review and remove adoption listings, messages, or profiles that violate platform rules or raise fraud concerns.
- ✅ **User management**: Administrators hold the authority to activate, suspend, or permanently remove user accounts if evidence of misuse appears.

## System Oversight
- ❌ **AI triage oversight**: Alongside verified veterinarians, administrators review and override Gemini's urgency classifications for reported cases.
- ✅ **Dispute resolution**: Administrators manually adjust reputation points or trust badges when disputes between users require intervention.
