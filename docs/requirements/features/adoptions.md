# Adoptions & User Onboarding

Every user joins the Happy Paws platform as a prospective adopter or owner. They can expand their capabilities over time by claiming new roles and completing identity verification.

## Account Management & Roles
- ✅ **Registration**: All users register for a base account. The platform provisions them as adopters initially.
- ✅ **Role assignment**: Users can assign themselves additional roles (Foster, Transporter, Veterinarian) from their dashboard. These elevated roles require submitting KYC documents for administrator review.

## Adoption Flow
- ✅ **Lifestyle profiles**: Adopters complete a profile covering home size, activity level, and existing pets. A rule-based matching engine suggests animals that fit their lifestyle.
- ✅ **Browsing**: Users can search and filter listed animals by species, location, and urgency, independent of the matching engine suggestions.
- ❌ **Applications**: Adopters submit applications for listed animals and track their status (pending, accepted, declined) from a centralized dashboard. The adopter's lifestyle profile automatically bundles with their application.
- ✅ **Rehoming listings**: Existing owners can post their own animals for rehoming using the same listing flow.

## Verification & Trust
- ✅ **KYC**: Users submit a national identity document for administrator review. Verification unlocks the ability to list animals, submit applications, and message freely.
- ❌ **Reputation**: Users accumulate reputation points and visible trust badges from confirmed activities, such as completed adoptions or verified rescue reports.
