# Sponsorship & Donations

Sponsors provide the financial backing needed to sustain rescue and foster operations. The platform offers tools to track where pledges go and how cases progress.

## Sponsoring Cases
- ❌ **Pledge support**: Verified sponsors can pledge support against a specific animal or rescue case. During the MVP phase, this is a simulated pledge rather than a live transaction.
- ❌ **Track progress**: Sponsors follow status updates on an animal or case they back directly from the screen used to make the original pledge.

## Transparency & Communication
- ❌ **Direct contact**: Sponsors can contact the foster, veterinarian, or administrator tied to a sponsored case for updates using private in-app messaging.
- ❌ **Reputation**: Sponsors build reputation and earn visible trust badges from confirmed, completed sponsorships so other users can see their support history.
