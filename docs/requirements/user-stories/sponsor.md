# Sponsor and donor functionalities

| User story | Functionality | Description | Completed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| As a sponsor, I want to log in to my sponsorship screens so I can track my cases. | Login and authentication | Signs in securely with role-scoped, JWT-enforced access to sponsorship and case-tracking screens. | ✅ |
| As a sponsor, I want to submit my identity document for review so I can pledge support against a case or listing. | Identity verification (KYC) | Submits an identity document for administrator review before the account can pledge support against a case or listing. | ✅ |
| As a sponsor, I want to browse rescue cases and adoption listings so I can decide who or what to support. | Browse rescue cases and adoption listings | Views triaged rescue cases and adoption listings, including the AI urgency classification attached to each one, to decide who or what to support. | ✅ |
| As a sponsor, I want to pledge support to a case or listing so I can record my sponsorship commitment. | Pledge support to a case or listing | Records a sponsorship commitment against a specific animal or case. In the three-week MVP this is a simulated pledge rather than a live transaction, consistent with the proposal’s decision to defer real payment processing to the post-MVP roadmap. | ❌ |
| As a sponsor, I want to track sponsored case progress so I can see what happened after my pledge. | Track sponsored case progress | Follows status updates on an animal or case the sponsor has backed, from the same screen used to make the original pledge. | ❌ |
| As a sponsor, I want to contact the foster, veterinarian, or administrator through private in-app messaging so I can get an update without sharing a phone number. | Private in-app messaging | Contacts the foster, veterinarian, or administrator attached to a sponsored case for an update, without sharing a phone number. | ❌ |
| As a sponsor, I want to earn reputation and trust badges from my completed sponsorships so other users can see my support history. | Reputation and trust badges | Builds reputation and a visible trust badge from confirmed, completed sponsorships. | ❌ |
| As a sponsor, I want to receive notifications for updates on sponsored cases so I don't have to check back manually. | Notifications | Receives alerts for updates on sponsored cases and incoming messages. | ❌ |
